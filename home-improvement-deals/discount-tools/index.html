Discount Tools: Where to Buy Quality at Lower Prices

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 6 min read

Quality tools do not need premium prices. Here is where to save.

Best sources

  • Hardware store scratch-and-dent — damaged packaging, perfect tool
  • Estate sales / estate sales apps — often free or $5 tools
  • Facebook Marketplace — used Cordless drills, saws
  • Holiday sales — Memorial Day, Labor Day, Black Friday
  • Refurbished — manufacturer-refurbished power tools

Essential toolkit (budget: $100–$200)

  • Claw hammer
  • Screwdriver set
  • Adjustable wrench
  • Pliers (needle-nose + channel-lock)
  • Tape measure
  • Level
  • Cordless drill
  • Utility knife

Brand priorities

Spend on: drill, saw, vacuum. Save on: hand tools, measurement.

Bottom line

A functional toolset costs $100–$200. See our DIY repairs guide to use them.
