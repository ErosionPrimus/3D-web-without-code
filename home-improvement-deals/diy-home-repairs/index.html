DIY Home Repairs Every Homeowner Should Know

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 8 min read

Calling a plumber or electrician for every issue costs thousands. Learn these repairs and save.

Fix a running toilet

Replace flapper ($5) — fixes 80% of running toilets. 15 minutes.

Unclog a drain

Plunger first, then drain snake ($10). Avoid chemical cleaners — they damage pipes.

Patch drywall

Small holes: spackle + sandpaper ($5). Large holes: patch kit ($15).

Reset circuit breaker

Identify tripped breaker → flip off → on. If it trips again, call electrician.

Replace faucet washer

Dripping faucet = worn washer ($2). Shut off water, disassemble, replace.

When to call a pro

  • Gas leaks — always call professional
  • Major electrical work
  • Roof structural issues
  • Foundation cracks wider than 1/4 inch

Bottom line

Basic DIY skills save $500–$2,000/year. Start with toilet and drain fixes.
