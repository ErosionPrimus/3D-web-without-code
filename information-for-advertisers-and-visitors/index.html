AdSense Optimization Tips for Family Sites

For publishers — maximize revenue while keeping user experience clean.

Ad placement best practices

  • Above-fold: 1 responsive leaderboard (728×90)
  • In-content: 1 rectangle (300×250) after first paragraph
  • Sidebar: 1 vertical rectangle (160×600)
  • Mobile: stick to 1–2 visible units

Content guidelines

  • Original content, 50%+ unique
  • Clear labeling of sponsored/promoted content
  • No clickbait or misleading titles
  • Functional design, no intrusive interstitials

What we do NOT do

  • No pop-ups or pop-unders
  • No auto-redirects
  • No fake "download" buttons
  • No doorway pages
