A Seasonal Garden Planning Calendar for Budget-Minded Families

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 10 min read

Gardening on a budget means planning around frost dates and sales — not spending on premium seedlings.

Spring (Mar–May)

  • Start seeds indoors 6–8 weeks before last frost
  • Prepare soil with compost (free/cheap)
  • Plant cool crops: peas, lettuce, spinach, radish

Summer (Jun–Aug)

  • Plant warm crops: tomatoes, peppers, beans, squash
  • Mulch heavily — suppresses weeds, retains moisture
  • Water deeply, early morning

Fall (Sep–Nov)

  • Plant second round of cool crops
  • Collect leaves for leaf mold
  • Plant cover crops in empty beds

Winter (Dec–Feb)

  • Plan next year's garden
  • Order seeds early for best selection
  • Maintain tools, repair beds

Budget tips

  • Seed swap with neighbors
  • Free compost from municipal programs
  • Save seeds from heirloom varieties
  • Repurpose containers (buckets, cans)

Bottom line

A planned garden costs $30–$60 first year and pays for itself in produce. See our first-year guide for details.
