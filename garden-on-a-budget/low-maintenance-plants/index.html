Low-Maintenance Garden Plants for Beginners

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 6 min read

Start with plants that thrive on neglect.

Vegetables

  • Zucchini — prolific, almost too much
  • Green beans — direct sow, minimal care
  • Tomatoes — stake and water
  • Radish — 30 days from seed to harvest

Herbs

  • Basil — grows like weed
  • Mint — invasive, plant in container
  • Rosemary — drought tolerant
  • Chives — perennial, come back yearly

Flowers

  • Marigolds — pest deterrent + color
  • Sunflowers — easy from seed
  • Zinnias — cut-and-come-again

Beginner tips

  • Water at base, not leaves
  • Mulch to retain moisture
  • Deadhead spent flowers
  • Observe daily — catch problems early

Bottom line
