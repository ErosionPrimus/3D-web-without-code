Cheap Healthy Meals: 10 Family Dinners Under $2 Per Serving

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 8 min read

Eating healthy on a budget is possible — here are ten proven dinner recipes.

1. Lentil soup + bread

Dry lentils, canned tomatoes, vegetables. ~$1.20/serving. Batch cook freezes well.

2. Rice and beans with salsa

Complete protein, ~$0.80/serving. Add cheese for kids.

3. Pasta with homemade marinara

Canned tomatoes, garlic, onion, dried herbs. ~$1.50/serving.

4. Oatmeal dinner (savory)

Oats + broth + topping. Budget staple repurposed. ~$0.70/serving.

5. Tacos with beans

Corn tortillas + seasoned black beans + veggies. ~$1.30/serving.

6. Stir-fry with eggs

Rice + frozen veg + eggs. ~$1.40/serving.

7. Baked potato bar

Baked potatoes + toppings (beans, cheese, salsa). ~$1.60/serving.

8. Chicken thighs + roasted veg

Bone-in thighs are cheapest cut. ~$2.00/serving.

9. Chickpea curry + rice

Canned chickpeas + coconut milk + spices. ~$1.50/serving.

10. Breakfast-for-dinner

Eggs, toast, fruit. Familiar to kids, cheap, fast. ~$1.00/serving.

Shopping tips

  • Buy dried beans, not canned (saves 60%)
  • Frozen vegetables are cheaper and last longer
  • Store brands for staples
  • Shop sales + use loyalty cards

Bottom line

Feeding a family of 4 for ~$10–$15/day is realistic with these recipes. See our grocery myths guide for more savings.
