Home First Aid Kit: What Families Actually Need

By FocusedBuzz Editorial · 6 min read

A well-stocked kit prevents $200 urgent-care visits for minor issues.

Essentials ($20–$40 total)

  • Adhesive bandages (assorted)
  • Gauze pads + medical tape
  • Antiseptic wipes
  • Antibiotic ointment
  • Tweezers + scissors
  • Thermometer
  • Pain relievers (acetaminophen/ibuprofen)
  • Hydrocortisone cream
  • Oral rehydration packets

Optional additions

  • EpiPen (if allergies — prescription)
  • CPR face shield
  • Emergency blanket
  • Instant cold packs

Maintenance

Check expiration dates every 6 months. Replace used items immediately.

Bottom line

$30 kit prevents hundreds in unnecessary urgent care. See our emergency fund guide for financial safety net.
